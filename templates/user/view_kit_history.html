Supplementary Nutrition Program (ICDS & Poshan Abhiyaan)

Registered pregnant and lactating mothers are entitled to take-home rations (THR) including fortified micro-nutrient flour (Amrutham Nutrimix), iron and folic acid supplements, and protein enrichments to ensure healthy maternal and fetal development.
